I just moved to Vilnius. How do I declare my residence?

Reading epaslaugos.lt · vilnius.lt

You can declare your place of residence at your ward office (seniūnija) or online through epaslaugos.lt. Bring your ID document; if you rent, the owner needs to give consent.
